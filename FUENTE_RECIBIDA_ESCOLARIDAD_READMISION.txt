2. Corroborating SHAP-based direction evidence (informs the same primary model)
A. Step-9 SHAP predictor/direction table — cons\prediction22_converted_mod.html (~line 6171) and its .ipynb:


Readmission | ed_attainment_corr | mean_abs_shap=0.0416 | rank 10 | direction_method=quartile_q4_minus_q1
  delta_point=-0.1052401 [CI -0.1057542, -0.1047200] | n=70369 | direction_label="Risk-Decreasing"
Method definition (found in XGboost_combined_feb26.ipynb, function direction_bootstrap_feature): compares mean SHAP value in the top quartile (Q4, high numeric-encoded value) vs. bottom quartile (Q1) of the feature. ed_attainment_corr's numeric encoding is the leading digit of the level string (confirmed via Duplicates_25_4.qmd line 2396: esc_num_corr = as.numeric(str_extract(ed_attainment_corr, "^[0-9]+"))), so Q4 = "3-Completed primary school or less" (least educated) and Q1 = "1-More than high school" (most educated). Negative delta ⇒ SHAP (and thus predicted hazard) is lower for the least-educated group than the most-educated group.

B. SHAP functional-form screen at the 60-month horizon — cons\_out\shap_60m_variable_summary.csv:


readmission,ed_attainment_c,...,feature_shap_corr=-0.8153790176,"strong_negative: higher feature values decrease SHAP"
death,     ed_attainment_c,...,feature_shap_corr= 0.9231435102,"strong_positive: higher feature values increase SHAP"
Same encoding direction as above (higher numeric value = less education). Readmission and mortality point in opposite directions: for readmission, less education → lower SHAP/risk; for mortality, less education → higher SHAP/risk. Caveat: ed_attainment_corr/ed_attainment is not in the final Cox death formula — confirmed by an explicit runtime log line in cons\prediction22_converted_mod.html (~line 11710): ">>> Skipping: ed_attainment - not in death formula <<<", and by _extract_formulas.log (formula_death_updated2's variable list does not include ed_attainment_corr). So the death-side SHAP signal reflects an exploratory/earlier XGBoost specification, not the final mortality Cox model.

cons\_out\shap_60m_functional_form_screen.csv / shap_60m_functional_form_recommendations.csv additionally show the linear/ordinal specification was judged adequate for readmission ("linear_or_current_dummy_form_sufficient"), consistent with a monotonic trend rather than a reversal across categories.
